WHAT I LIKE ABOUT NIGERIA

RESILENCE
Nigerians possess a famous survivor and problem-solving mentality, turning challenges into oppourtunities and bending without breaking.
INCREDIBLE FOOD
Nigerian food is legendary, anchored by word world-famous dishes like smoky party jollof rice, pounded yam, and a vast array of flavorful regional soups.
BOLD ENTERPRENEURIAL DRIVE
An unstoppable business mindset where everyday people turn ideas, side hustle, and tech innovation into thriving ventures.
CULTURAL DIVERSITY
Home to over 250 ethics groups and more than 500 spoken languages.
LAND OF INVENTION AND INVENTORS
Nigeria has produced great inventors over the years and there are over 40 different types of solid minerals alongside major fossil fuel reserves.
