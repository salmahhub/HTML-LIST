STEPS IN PREPARING MY BEST FOOD
1. On the fire
2. Rinse the pot and put it on the fire
3. Pour some water inside the pot
4. Rinse and Pour the neat beans after the water boils
5. Leave it to cook for some minutes and add the peeled already cut onions
6. After the beans has been cooked for a period of time, add the pepper, the palm oil, and some salt
7. Leave the food till the ingredients cooked well with it
8. Off the gas after some minutes and dish out the food
